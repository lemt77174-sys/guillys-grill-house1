PROPOSED SURIGAO CITY EXPANSION
PLEASE NOTE: This website presents a market-development concept. The Surigao City branch/franchise is proposed and is not represented as currently operating.
01 / The concept

Our grill-house spirit

A familiar brand, grilled favorites, and a place to gather. The expansion concept keeps the existing Guilly's identity while planning for a new local market.

01 — BUSINESS CONCEPT

Guilly's Grill House

A casual grill-house dining concept associated with rice meals, grilled favorites, and selected steak dishes. The supplied original black-and-white logo is used as the visual reference.

02 — TARGET CUSTOMERS

For different occasions

Students and young adults, employees, families, groups of friends, visitors, and food enthusiasts looking for convenient meals and a comfortable place to eat.

03 — LOCATION

Surigao City, Philippines

A commercial area near offices, retail, homes, or transport routes is being considered. Final site selection depends on foot traffic, rent, access, and competition.

04 — CUSTOMER NEED

Meals worth gathering for

The concept aims to offer convenient, satisfying meals for everyday dining and group occasions, bringing a known Butuan brand closer to Surigao customers.

05 — PRODUCT-MARKET FIT

Familiar menu, local insight

Keep recognizable menu favorites and study local preferences before changing portions, bundles, or specials. Customer feedback will guide adaptation.

06 — VALUE PROPOSITION

Familiar favorites, closer

Enjoy Guilly's Grill House's existing food concept in a welcoming setting, with meal options intended to suit solo diners and groups.

02 / Market strategy

How we'll enter the market

Expansion should be based on research and approved operating plans—not assumptions. These are the proposed strategies for the Surigao market.

07 — SEGMENTATION

Know the audience

  • Geographic: Surigao City and nearby communities
  • Demographic: students, workers, adults, families, visitors
  • Psychographic: grill-food fans and social diners
  • Behavioral: dine-in, takeout, and group-order customers
08 — ADVANTAGE

Build on the brand

Use existing brand recognition, a recognizable menu, consistent preparation, welcoming service, and reliable quality. Validate differences against local competitors.

09 — POSITIONING

Good food, good company

A familiar grill-house brand from Butuan aiming to become a trusted dining option in Surigao City for flavorful meals and group gatherings.

10 — MARKET ENTRY

Research, prepare, launch

Survey customers, compare sites and competitors, secure approvals, prepare staff and suppliers, then introduce the branch through a measured launch campaign.

12 — DISTRIBUTION

Easy ways to order

Planned channels include dine-in, takeout, direct online inquiries, group orders, and delivery where locally available and financially viable.

13 — PROMOTION

Meet the community

Possible activities include opening-week deals, group bundles, posters, local food creators, loyalty offers, and community partnerships after launch approval.

04 / Proposed expansion roadmap

From idea to opening

The proposed geographic market development plan takes the Guilly's Grill House concept from Butuan into a new city, subject to business approval and feasibility.

01 / RESEARCH

Survey potential customers, map competitors, compare rental sites, and estimate demand and costs.

02 / PREPARE

Confirm branch or franchise authorization, permits, site, suppliers, equipment, and staff training.

03 / INTRODUCE

Publish official announcements, menu details, opening offers, and accurate location information once confirmed.

04 / IMPROVE

Track sales, repeat visits, customer feedback, food waste, and promotion results; improve from evidence.

11 — Pricing approach

Value with a clear basis

Use cost-based pricing supported by competitor research and customer value. Any menu prices shown here are reference examples only; final Surigao pricing depends on actual costs and approved menu details.

14 — DIGITAL MARKETING

Make it easy to discover us

Plan for a local Facebook presence, short TikTok grilling videos, Instagram food photography, online inquiries, and a QR code linking to the verified menu or ordering page. Publish official details only after confirmation.

05 / Responsible growth

Built to last

Growth is meaningful when it works for customers, employees, local suppliers, and the business over time.

15 — INNOVATION

Convenience, thoughtfully

Potential improvements include digital menus, organized online ordering, group meal packages, customer surveys, and limited-time specials tested against demand and cost.

16 — SUSTAINABILITY

Responsible operations

Plan portions to reduce food waste, manage energy and packaging, train and hire local workers where possible, maintain safe food handling, and source from qualified local suppliers when practical.

LOCAL MARKET

Earn trust locally

Listen to Surigao customers and neighbors, maintain transparent promotions, and build a reputation through consistent food and service rather than promises alone.
